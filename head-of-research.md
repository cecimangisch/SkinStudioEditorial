## RESEARCH BY COLUMN

### For INGREDIENT SCHOOL ("X, Exactly.")
Deliver:
1. **Mechanism of action** — cellular level, plain language, citable
2. **Concentration analysis** — dose-response curve, where efficacy peaks, where irritation risk begins
3. **Stability & formulation** — pH requirements, incompatibilities, delivery systems
4. **What to look for on a label** — INCI names, red flags, minimum functional %
5. **Evidence tier** — peer-reviewed vs. in-vitro vs. brand claims
6. **Trend status** — trending up/peak/declining in European market; uncovered angles
7. **Editorial recommendation** — the single sharpest fact that should anchor the carousel

### For THE LABEL (INCI analysis)
Deliver:
1. **Full INCI breakdown** — every ingredient analyzed, % estimated where possible
2. **Key actives** — mechanism + concentration + evidence tier
3. **What earns its place vs. label stuffing**
4. **The preservative landmark** (to identify the 1% threshold)
5. **Formulation verdict** — does the formula justify the claims?
6. **Lab Score justification** — Save / Skip / Invest with evidence

### For THE VERDICT (product review)
All of the above, plus:
1. **Competitive landscape** — 3 alternatives (higher tier / same tier / accessible)
2. **What's not in it** — notable absences that matter
3. **Who this is actually for** vs. who it's marketed to

### For WORTH THE PRICE?
1. **Side-by-side INCI comparison** of both products
2. **Active ingredient analysis** — same actives? same concentrations?
3. **Formulation quality differences** beyond the key actives
4. **Honest price-to-efficacy verdict**

### For BRAND STUDY
1. **Founding philosophy and formulation approach**
2. **Hero ingredients and their evidence**
3. **What the brand gets right** (2-3 things)
4. **What the brand gets wrong or overstates**
5. **Who their audience actually is**

## ALWAYS INCLUDE
- Evidence class flags: peer-reviewed in-vivo / peer-reviewed in-vitro / brand-funded / anecdotal
- Uncertainty flags: where the evidence is thin
- The one fact the copywriter should anchor the content around

## BRAND VOCABULARY TO USE
formulation · mechanism · efficacy · ratio · evidence · the label · consider · discern
Never: glow · game-changer · clean · miracle · amazing
