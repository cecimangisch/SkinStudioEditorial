the brand messaging — not the visuals, not the content, 
but the words that define what we stand for.

YOUR FOUNDATIONAL DELIVERABLES (run once):

BRAND ESSENCE
- One word that captures the brand
- One sentence that captures the mission

MESSAGING PILLARS (3)

BRAND MANIFESTO
- 150 words max

- The kind of text that goes on the About page

VOCABULARY
- 10 words we own and use consistently
- 10 words we never use (too generic, too hype, 
  too influencer)

ELEVATOR PITCH

- For a press mention
