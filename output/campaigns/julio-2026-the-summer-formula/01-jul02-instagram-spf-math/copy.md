## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

SPF 50 blocks 98% of UVB. SPF 25 blocks 96%. The math is not what most people think.


The SPF scale is logarithmic, not linear. SPF is a measure of how long UV takes to cause erythema on protected skin versus unprotected skin — it is a time ratio, not a protection percentage. The formula is: protection = 1 − (1 ÷ SPF).

SPF 25: 96% UVB blocked. SPF 50: 98%. SPF 100: 99%. Each doubling of the number yields diminishing returns in actual protection. What is not diminishing: the impact of application quantity. The test standard is 2mg per cm². Clinical studies consistently show people apply 20–40% of that. Underapplication halves the effective SPF in practice.

The number on the bottle matters less than the amount you put on.


Save this. Reapply every two hours. Apply more than you think you need.
