## Slide 1

**Canvas:** 1080×1080px
**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8
**Layout:** Centered typographic composition. No photography.

**Typography:**
- Line 1: "SPF 50 doesn't mean" — DM Sans Regular, 48px, Ink #1A1A18
- Line 2: "twice the protection" — Fraunces Regular, 72px, Ink #1A1A18 [SPOTLIGHT]
- Line 3: "of SPF 25." — DM Sans Regular, 48px, Ink #1A1A18
- Gap of 60px
- Line 4: "Here is what the number actually measures." — DM Sans Regular, 28px, Ink #1A1A18, 70% opacity
- Bottom right: "@skinlabeditorial" — DM Sans Regular, 20px, Ink #1A1A18, 50% opacity

**Design Notes:**
- No rules, no borders, no decorative elements
- Text block vertically centered with slight upward offset (center of mass at 45% of height)
- "twice the protection" on its own line — the contrast between DM Sans and Fraunces is the only visual event on this slide
- Generous whitespace

## Slide 2

**Canvas:** 1080×1080px
**Background:** Ink #1A1A18
**Text color:** Bone #F5F0E8
**Layout:** Two-column typographic layout. Left column: labels. Right column: values. No photography.

**Typography:**
- Headline: "The SPF scale is not linear." — DM Sans Bold, 38px, Bone #F5F0E8
- Subhead separator: thin 1px rule in Lab Sage #BBD1C6, full width, 20px margin
- Data rows (DM Sans Regular, 26px):
  - "SPF 25 ............... 96% UVB blocked"
  - "SPF 50 ............... 98% UVB blocked"
  - "SPF 100 ............. 99% UVB blocked"
- Formula block: DM Sans Regular, 22px, Lab Mist #DAE9DF — "protection = 1 − (1 ÷ SPF)"
- Body text: DM Sans Regular, 22px, Bone #F5F0E8, 80% opacity, 680px wide block
- Footer: "Ingredients, decoded. · @skinlabeditorial" — DM Sans Regular, 18px, Bone #F5F0E8, 40% opacity

**Design Notes:**
- The formula should sit in a subtle Lab Mist #DAE9DF/10% tinted box — just enough to distinguish it as a distinct element
- Tight leading on body text (1.4x)
- No decorative elements beyond the single rule
- Bottom footer pinned to 40px from bottom edge
