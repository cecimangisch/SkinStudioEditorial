## TIKTOK CAPTION

Every June, the summer skincare routine edit appears. New moisturiser. Lighter serum. Summer-specific SPF. A new set of products for a new season.

What summer actually changes: UV increases active ingredient degradation (Vitamin C oxidises faster). Heat affects formulation stability. Sweat alters product adhesion. These are real variables — but they affect how your existing products perform, not what product category you need.

The "summer routine" as a distinct purchase event is a marketing construct. The skincare industry benefits from seasonal reinvention. Your skin does not require it.

What it does require: understanding the conditions. And adjusting accordingly.
