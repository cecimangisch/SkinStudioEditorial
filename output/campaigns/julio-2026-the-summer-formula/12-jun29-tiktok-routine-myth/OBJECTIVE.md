## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

The "summer skincare routine" is a marketing category. It generates product sales by implying that summer requires a different set of products — a lighter moisturiser, a summer serum, a new cleanser. What summer actually changes is not the product need, but the conditions under which your existing products work: UV increases active degradation, heat affects formulation stability, sweat changes product adhesion, and increased sun exposure shifts priorities toward barrier integrity and UV protection.

The provocation: your routine doesn't need replacing. Your understanding of what UV and heat do to the products you already use does.

**What the reader must feel:** Mild disruption of a seasonal buying reflex. The desire to follow because this account saves them money and confusion.
