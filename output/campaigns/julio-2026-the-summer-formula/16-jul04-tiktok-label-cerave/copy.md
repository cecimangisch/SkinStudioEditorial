## TIKTOK CAPTION

CeraVe AM Moisturising Lotion SPF 30 is one of the most purchased SPF products on the market. The ceramide + sunscreen positioning is everywhere. The actual formula breakdown is not.

UV filter system: Homosalate, Octisalate, Octocrylene. Chemical filters, strong UVB coverage. UVA protection is adequate for daily use — not the full-spectrum coverage you get from Mexoryl or Tinosorb systems.

Ceramides NP, AP, and EOP are present — all three of the most documented types. They appear mid-list, which means this reads primarily as an SPF product with ceramide support. That is still good. It is not a ceramide-first formula.

Lab Score: SAVE. The best-value ceramide-plus-SPF combination on the market. Not a substitute for dedicated high-UV-exposure sunscreen.
