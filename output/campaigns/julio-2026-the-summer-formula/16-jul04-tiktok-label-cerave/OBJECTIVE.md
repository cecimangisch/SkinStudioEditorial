## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

CeraVe AM Moisturising Lotion SPF 30 is one of the highest-volume SPF-moisturiser combination products in the European and US market. Its reputation rests on the ceramide + SPF promise. This audit asks: does the formula deliver on that promise?

Findings: SPF filters are chemical (Homosalate, Octisalate, Octocrylene) — solid UVB coverage, UVA coverage functional but not best-in-class. Three ceramides present (CER NP, AP, EOP) but positioned mid-list — present at meaningful levels, but this is primarily an SPF product with ceramide support, not a ceramide product with SPF. Niacinamide appears low on the list. Lab Score: SAVE.

**What the reader must feel:** That they understand precisely what they are buying — a good, accessible SPF-moisturiser combination — and can make an informed decision about whether it meets their specific need.
