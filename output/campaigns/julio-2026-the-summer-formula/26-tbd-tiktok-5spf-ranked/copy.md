## TIKTOK CAPTION

We ranked 5 pharmacy SPFs by formula quality, not price. Here's what the INCI lists actually say.

Budget tier: Garnier Sensitive Advanced (~€12) beats Altruist (€6.50/100ml) — three filters vs two, including Mexoryl XL. The difference in price is real on paper. But note: Altruist gives you 100ml for €6.50. Full dose, every time.

Mid tier: Anthelios UVMune 400 (€20/50ml) beats Eucerin (~€18) — Mexoryl 400 covers the full UVA spectrum to 400nm, the deepest coverage in this group and in pharmacy SPF generally.

ISDIN Fusion Fluid (~€28) is the most expensive and ranks second. Excellent four-filter system and premium texture — but Anthelios costs less and goes deeper on UVA.

Formula rank: Anthelios 9.3 · ISDIN 9.0 · Eucerin 8.8 · Garnier 8.6 · Altruist 8.5. The ranking is not linear with price. It never is.
