## TIKTOK CAPTION

We ranked 5 pharmacy SPFs by formula quality, not price. Here's what the INCI lists actually say.

Budget tier: Garnier Sensitive Advanced (€12) beats Altruist (€8) — three filters vs two, including Mexoryl XL. The €4 difference is real on paper.

Mid tier: Anthelios (€22) beats Eucerin (€18) — Mexoryl SX extends UVA1 coverage to 340nm, the deepest in this group.

ISDIN Fusion Fluid (€28) is the most expensive and ranks second. The four-filter system is excellent, the texture is premium — but the Mexoryl SX gap puts Anthelios above it.

Formula rank: Anthelios 9.2 · ISDIN 9.0 · Eucerin 8.8 · Garnier 8.6 · Altruist 8.5. The ranking is not linear with price. It never is.
