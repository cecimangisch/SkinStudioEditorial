## SLIDE 3 — INCI Analysis: UV Filters

**Background:** Ink #1A1A18
**Text:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** The UV filters.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
**Zinc Oxide** — primary UV filter. Broad-spectrum: UVB, UVA2, UVA1. Photostable. Anti-inflammatory secondary effect.

**Titanium Dioxide** — secondary UV filter. Excellent UVB coverage, weaker UVA1. Works synergistically with zinc oxide to extend spectrum.

Combination mineral filtering is standard in high-performance mineral sunscreens. Both filters are EU and FDA approved. Both are photostable. The dual-filter approach allows lower individual concentrations, which reduces whitening.

Assessment: UV filter system is appropriate and effective for SPF 50 claim.

## SLIDE 4 — INCI Analysis: Skin Conditioners

**Background:** Lab Mist #DAE9DF

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** The moisturising base.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
**Glycerin** — humectant. Draws water to the stratum corneum. Standard, effective, well-tolerated.

**Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride** — emollient derived from coconut oil. Lightweight, non-comedogenic. Improves spreadability of mineral filters.

**Panthenol (Vitamin B5)** — skin-conditioning agent with minor anti-inflammatory effect. Standard in sensitive-skin formulations.

**Allantoin** — soothing agent. Reduces transepidermal water loss. Well-documented for skin tolerance.

Assessment: moisturising base is functional and sensitive-skin appropriate. No red flags.

---

## SLIDE 5 — INCI Analysis: Emulsifiers and Stabilisers

**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** The emulsifiers and stabilisers.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
**Cetearyl Alcohol / Ceteareth-20** — fatty alcohol / emulsifier pair. Stabilise the oil-water emulsion. Cetearyl alcohol is a skin-conditioning emollient, not an irritant despite the "alcohol" name.

**Dimethicone** — silicone emollient. Improves texture and slip. Reduces the chalky drag associated with high-percentage mineral formulas.

**Phenoxyethanol / Ethylhexylglycerin** — preservative system. Industry standard. Concentration-dependent safety profile is well-established.

Assessment: stabiliser system is unremarkable and appropriate. No fragrance listed.

## SLIDE 6 — INCI Analysis: What's not in it

**Background:** Linen #F1EAE1

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** What is not in the formula.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
No chemical UV filters (no oxybenzone, no avobenzone, no octinoxate).
No added fragrance or parfum.
No alcohol denat.
No known irritating botanical extracts.

This is a clean-architecture formula. For sensitive, reactive, or rosacea-prone skin, the absence of fragrance and chemical filters is clinically meaningful — not a marketing claim.

## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

€4 mineral SPF 50. The formulation actually holds up.


Altruist Mineral SPF 50 uses zinc oxide and titanium dioxide as its UV filters — a dual-mineral combination that covers UVB, UVA2, and UVA1. Both filters are photostable. No chemical UV filters, no added fragrance, no alcohol denat.

The moisturising base is glycerin, caprylic/capric triglyceride, panthenol, and allantoin. Functional, not decorative. The emulsifier system uses cetearyl alcohol and dimethicone — which is why this formula applies more smoothly than most budget mineral SPFs.

Verdict: the packaging claims are supported by the formula. The only genuine limitation is whitening on deeper skin tones, which is a titanium dioxide particle size issue, not a safety concern.

Lab score: 8.5 / 10. Worth It.
