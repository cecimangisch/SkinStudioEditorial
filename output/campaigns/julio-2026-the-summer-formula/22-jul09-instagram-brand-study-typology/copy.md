## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Typology is a French skincare brand with one unusual practice: they publish the percentage of every active ingredient on the label and their website. In a category where most brands list "niacinamide" without telling you whether it's 2% or 10%, this is a structural editorial choice.

Founded in Paris in 2019. No fragrance as a default formulation decision. Clinical testing across the range. Price point €10–25.

The question a brand study must ask: does the transparency hold up? Largely, yes. Their niacinamide is at 10% (above the 5% clinical threshold). Their retinol at 0.5% (effective mid-range). The limitation is real: some actives sit toward the lower end of the clinically studied concentration range. But the transparency model means you can identify that yourself and make an informed decision.

That is the point of the brand.

Lab Score: WORTH STUDYING. Rare in the category. Evidence-aware. A model for what ingredient disclosure could look like if more brands did it.
