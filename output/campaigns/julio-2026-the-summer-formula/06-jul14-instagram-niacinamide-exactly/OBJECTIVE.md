# OBJECTIVE — 06 · Niacinamide, Exactly.
## Ingredient School · July 14, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
Niacinamide is one of the most discussed skincare ingredients. It is also one of the most misrepresented. This post explains the full mechanism: ceramide synthesis, sebum regulation, anti-inflammatory effect via cytokine modulation, and the melanin transfer inhibition mechanism that makes it effective for hyperpigmentation. The summer angle: niacinamide's barrier-supportive and sebum-regulating effects make it particularly relevant in conditions of heat and humidity. Authority content.

**Editorial Note:**
The "shrinks pores" claim is the entry point because it is the most commonly believed misconception. Pores do not have muscles. They cannot open or close. Niacinamide reduces the appearance of pores by regulating sebum and improving skin texture — a different mechanism than "shrinking."
