## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Niacinamide is one of the most marketed ingredients in skincare. It is also one of the most misrepresented.

The clinical evidence is built on 5% concentration. The jump to 10% is a market convention — the evidence for additional benefit above 5% is not well-established.

What the research does support: inhibition of melanosome transfer (relevant in summer, when UV increases transfer rate), sebum modulation at cellular level, ceramide synthesis stimulation, and reduced TEWL. Three separate mechanisms. All documented.

In summer specifically: stability matters. Unlike Vitamin C, niacinamide does not degrade in heat or UV exposure. It works when conditions are difficult for other actives.

Read the label. Look for 5%. Combine with SPF.

Save this for the next time someone tries to sell you 10%.
