## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Every year in June: the summer skincare routine edit. New textures. New formats. A seasonal product refresh framed as necessity.

What summer actually changes is not the category of product you need — it is the conditions your existing products work in. UV increases the rate at which unstable actives degrade. Heat affects the structural integrity of some formulations. Increased sweat changes how products adhere to and penetrate the skin. These are real variables. But they are conditions to understand, not gaps to fill with new purchases.

The "summer routine" as a distinct buying moment exists because it generates sales. Not because your skin requires a new set of products every June.

Understand the conditions. Adjust accordingly. Save the money.
