## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Altruist SPF 50 costs ~€8. La Roche-Posay Anthelios costs ~€22. Neither dominates unconditionally.

Altruist's UVA protection is not weak — it publishes a PPD of 52 (5-star ultra-UVA), which exceeds EU standards and outperforms many SPFs at any price. Tinosorb S and Tinosorb M provide strong, photostable broad-spectrum coverage. Add Panthenol (Provitamin B5) for post-UV soothing, water resistance, and a dermatologist-formulated base. For €8, the formula is not cutting corners.

Anthelios's edge is specific: Mexoryl SX extends UVA coverage into the 340–400nm range (UVA1) — the band most associated with photoaging and immunosuppression. Altruist's broad UVA profile is strong; it just doesn't go as deep into that specific range. Mexoryl XL improves photostability, and the silica + dimethicone system is built for midday wearability.

Then there's the dosing argument. SPF testing assumes 2mg/cm² — about ¼ teaspoon for the face. At €8, you apply without hesitation. Studies show most people apply 20–50% of the recommended dose. Underapply Anthelios and SPF 50 behaves closer to SPF 7.

Altruist: 8.5/10 — PPD 52, strong broad-spectrum, Panthenol, best for generous use. Anthelios: 9.2/10 — deepest UVA1 coverage, compliance-engineered, best formula if applied correctly.
