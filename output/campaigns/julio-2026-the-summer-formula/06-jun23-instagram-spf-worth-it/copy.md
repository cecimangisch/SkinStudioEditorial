At €6.50/100ml, you apply the full 2mg/cm² recommended dose without hesitation. Most people underapply expensive SPF — at half dose, SPF 50 performs closer to SPF 7. That gap is real and rarely discussed.

Anthelios UVMune 400: 9.3/10 — the most advanced UVA protection in pharmacy SPF, for people who apply correctly. Altruist: 8.5/10 — PPD 52, ultra high UVA, and the product people will actually use at the right dose.
