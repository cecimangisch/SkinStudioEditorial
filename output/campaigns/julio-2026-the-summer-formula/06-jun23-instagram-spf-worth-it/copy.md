## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Altruist SPF 50 costs €8. La Roche-Posay Anthelios costs €45. The UV filters are not meaningfully different.

Both formulas use Tinosorb S and Tinosorb M — broad-spectrum, photostable, EU-approved UV filters. Anthelios adds Mexoryl SX and XL for extended UVA1 coverage, plus silica for sebum absorption. That silica is where the premium goes. It changes how the formula wears — lighter, drier, less likely to migrate.

The compliance argument: a sunscreen that disappears at midday because it feels heavy stops protecting at midday. If the more expensive formula improves compliance, the price differential has a real-world case. That is not a reason to dismiss the €8 option — it is a reason to understand why the €45 one exists.

Protection efficacy: equivalent. Cosmetic experience: meaningfully different. Choose based on how you actually behave with each one.
