## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Ceramides appear on barrier creams, serums, and moisturisers across every price point. Almost no marketing explains what they are or what they actually do.

They are sphingolipids — lipid molecules that form the lamellar bilayer structure of the stratum corneum. This structure is the barrier: the physical architecture that controls transepidermal water loss and filters what enters the skin.

UV radiation (specifically UVB) reduces ceramide synthesis and degrades existing lipids in the matrix. This is why summer barrier damage is a ceramide-depletion event, not just a dryness event. Humectants address hydration. They do not rebuild lipid structure. Ceramide replenishment does.

What to look for: Ceramide NP, Ceramide AP, Ceramide EOP — ideally all three, positioned in the first half of the INCI list.

Save this for the next time you read a label.
