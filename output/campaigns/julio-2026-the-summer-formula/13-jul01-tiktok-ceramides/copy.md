## TIKTOK CAPTION

Ceramides are the lipid mortar between skin cells. They make up approximately 50% of the stratum corneum. UV radiation — specifically UVB — reduces ceramide synthesis and degrades the lipids already present.

When ceramide levels drop, the barrier becomes more permeable. TEWL increases. Skin becomes more reactive to temperature, actives, and environmental stress. This is not a cosmetic problem. It is a structural one.

What to look for on the label: Ceramide NP (the most clinically studied), Ceramide AP, Ceramide EOP. All three together is the standard for barrier-repair formulations.
