# OBJECTIVE — 08 · Ceramides, Exactly.
## Ingredient School · July 18, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
Ceramides are the most structurally important lipids in the skin's barrier — yet most consumers who use ceramide products do not understand what they are or how they function. This post explains the complete picture: what ceramides are structurally, how they function in the lipid bilayer model of the stratum corneum, how they deplete in summer conditions, and what topical ceramides actually do in a formulation. **Editorial Note:**
The structural framing is the editorial angle: ceramides are not added to skin to make it more hydrated in a direct sense. They restore the lipid bilayer architecture of the stratum corneum, which then reduces TEWL, which then improves hydration. The mechanism is architectural, not simply moisturising. This distinction is worth making clearly.
