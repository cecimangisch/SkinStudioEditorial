## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Most mineral SPFs leave a white cast. This one doesn't. The chemistry behind that is worth understanding.

Colorescience Total Protection No-Show uses zinc oxide (10%) and titanium dioxide (8%) as its actives — both mineral, genuinely broad spectrum. The "no-show" comes from iron oxides in the formula: they absorb the visible wavelengths that cause white cast, and they happen to be the same pigments that provide blue light absorption. That claim is supported by the evidence.

The pollution defense claim is where the formula overreaches. There are antioxidants present — a Vitamin C derivative, Niacinamide — that support skin resilience. But no topical SPF has been shown to provide discrete pollution protection in controlled conditions. The physical barrier helps. The marketing language goes further than the data does.

Filters, iron oxide technology, antioxidant support: all solid. Lab Score: Invest — if invisible mineral is the brief, the formulation justifies the price.
