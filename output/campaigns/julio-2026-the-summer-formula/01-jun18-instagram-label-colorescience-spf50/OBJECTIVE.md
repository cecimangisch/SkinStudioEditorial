## Editorial Objective

This is the first product INCI audit of the campaign. The Colorescience No-Show makes three claims beyond SPF: 100% mineral, invisible on all skin tones, and protection against blue light and pollution. The first two are verifiable from the formulation. The second two are where the editorial work is.

The angle is not "this is good" or "this is bad." The angle is: here is what each claim means chemically, and here is what the label actually supports. The reader leaves knowing how to assess any SPF that makes the same claims.

**The one thing the audience must feel:** equipped — like they can now read any mineral SPF label and know what they're actually buying.
