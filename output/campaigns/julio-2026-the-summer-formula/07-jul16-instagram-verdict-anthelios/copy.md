## SLIDE 4 — Texture and formula notes: reapplication

**Background:** Linen #F1EAE1

**Headline:** The reapplication problem.

**Body:**
The formula's fluid consistency makes first application easy. Reapplication over makeup is the harder problem — applicable to all SPFs, not specific to Anthelios.

At 50ml for €45, this is not a product most users will apply generously twice daily outdoors. Budget planning matters: at proper outdoor use (face + décolletage, 2mg/cm², twice), a 50ml bottle lasts approximately 10 days of heavy use.

This is not a criticism of the product. It is a real cost consideration that changes the value calculation.

## SLIDE 5 — Ingredient analysis: UV filters

**Background:** Ink #1A1A18
**Text:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** The UV filters.

**Body:**
**Mexoryl SX (ecamsule):** L'Oréal-patented photostable UVA filter. Covers UVA1 and UVA2. One of the most effective single UVA filters available. Not replicable by competitors without licensing.

**Mexoryl XL (drometrizole trisiloxane):** Hybrid filter — covers both UVA and UVB. Oil-soluble. Photostable.

**Tinosorb S (bis-ethylhexyloxyphenol methoxyphenyl triazine):** Broad-spectrum, photostable. Also stabilises avobenzone.

**Avobenzone:** UVA1 filter. Photounstable on its own — stabilised here by Tinosorb S.

Filter assessment: outstanding. Four complementary filters providing complete, photostable broad-spectrum coverage.

No fragrance. No alcohol denat. No known irritants at listed concentrations.

## SLIDE 7 — Ingredient analysis: what the formula does well

**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** What the formula does well.

**Body:**
The Mexoryl SX + XL + Tinosorb S combination achieves something most SPF formulas do not: comprehensive, photostable UVA1 coverage without trade-offs in aesthetics.

Most SPFs that cover UVA1 adequately do so with mineral filters (zinc oxide) — and the aesthetic trade-off is whitening and texture. Anthelios achieves equivalent UVA1 coverage with a texture that disappears on skin.

This is the formulation achievement. It is real, it is patented, and it is why this product appears repeatedly in dermatologist recommendations.

## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Mexoryl SX is patented by L'Oréal. Most sunscreens don't have it. Here is what that means in practice.


Mexoryl SX (ecamsule) is a photostable UVA filter that covers the deep UVA1 range — the spectrum between 340–400nm where most available SPF formulas underperform. It is not in most sunscreens because it is under L'Oréal patent. In the Anthelios Invisible Fluid, it is paired with Mexoryl XL, Tinosorb S, and avobenzone for a four-filter broad-spectrum system with no photostability gaps.

Texture: fluid, invisible on all skin tones, no fragrance. The 2mg/cm² dosing standard is achievable without effort.

The limitations are real: 50ml at €45 is expensive for proper daily outdoor use. Methylparaben is present (within EU limits, but relevant if you prefer paraben-free). Octocrylene is present — patch test if you have sensitisation history.
