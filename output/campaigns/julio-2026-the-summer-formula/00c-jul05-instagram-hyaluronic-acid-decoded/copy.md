## Instagram Caption

Hyaluronic acid is not one ingredient. It is a category.

Most labels won't tell you which version you're getting. But the INCI list will — if you know what to look for. Molecular weight determines where in the skin HA works, and "hydrolyzed" on the label usually means smaller molecules, which penetrate further.

The evidence for surface-level HA is strong and well-established. For low-molecular-weight penetration: emerging, not conclusive. Both are useful. Neither requires an expensive product.

What to look for: HA listed in the top third of the INCI. An occlusive in the same formulation. The word "hydrolyzed" if you want deeper-layer delivery.

What to ignore: the marketing copy on the front of the bottle.
