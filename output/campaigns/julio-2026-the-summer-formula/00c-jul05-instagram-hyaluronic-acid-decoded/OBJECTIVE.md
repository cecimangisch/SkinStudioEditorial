## Editorial Objective

The first Ingredient School post of the account. Hyaluronic acid was chosen deliberately: it is the most over-marketed ingredient in the category, and it is genuinely useful — but only when you understand the mechanism. The disclosure gap (the fact that "hyaluronic acid" is not a single molecule but a category of molecules at different molecular weights) is the hook.

This post teaches the reader one thing that changes how they read every HA product label from now on: look for molecular weight, and look for the word "hydrolyzed."

That is the editorial value. Not "HA is good." That is marketing.
