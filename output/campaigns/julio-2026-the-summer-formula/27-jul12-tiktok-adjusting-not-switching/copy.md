## TIKTOK CAPTION

You don't need a different skincare routine in summer. You need to adjust how you use the one you have.

Here's why: UV exposure degrades ceramides in the stratum corneum — the lipid barrier holding your skin together. When ceramide levels drop, two things follow: transepidermal water loss increases, and sebum production increases in compensation. Neither of these is addressed by buying lighter products or a seasonal edit.

Adjustments instead: Apply barrier-supporting products more frequently. Your skin is working harder to compensate for UV-induced barrier stress — support it twice daily, not just evening. Time photosensitive ingredients correctly. Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) oxidises in light. Morning application means your serum is degraded before it penetrates. Evening application — when light exposure ends — lets it work.

Same products. Different frequency, timing, and logic.

The summer routine edit is a marketing format. The actual intervention is understanding the conditions — and adjusting the application of what you already own.
