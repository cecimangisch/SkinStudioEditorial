## TIKTOK CAPTION

We audited 13 verified May-June 2026 skincare launches by reading every INCI list. Here's what the formulas actually say — and which ones nailed the formula-to-benefit math.

The failures: Obagi, Lancôme, INKEY List, Jurlique all brought real innovation but formula gaps showed. Obagi's vitamin C lacks photostable antioxidant backup (no ferulic + E for summer). Lancôme split actives across three products (less concentration per step). INKEY's oat balm prioritizes texture over true occlusion. Jurlique stayed botanical, skipped clinical depth.

The winners: The Ordinary (Caffeine 3% + Escin 1% at $9–14 — every dollar to actives). Cetaphil AM/PM serums ($19.99 each — separated day/night so actives don't conflict). Three sunscreen innovations: Botnia Mineral (no white cast via spherical silica), EADEM Sunsuede Stick (reapply over makeup without disruption), ILIA Sun Serum (tinted mineral serum in one step). PCA SKIN Triple Exfoliation (BHA + AHA + PHA for three distinct exfoliation angles). Augustinus Bader, La Roche-Posay Anthelios (photostable, barrier-conscious, sun-ready).

The pattern: Formula-first wins. Photostability is table stakes. Sunscreen is non-negotiable. Before buying the next hero product, audit the INCI — because the formula proves it.
