## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Your summer skin isn't oilier. The barrier is more compromised.

UV radiation accelerates ceramide degradation in the stratum corneum. Ceramides are the lipid molecules that form the barrier — and when UV depletes them, transepidermal water loss increases regardless of how humid the air feels.

The perceived oiliness is sebum production responding to heat. The actual structural problem is a compromised lipid bilayer. These are different mechanisms. The response to one does not address the other.

Summer skin needs barrier support. Not necessarily lighter products.
