## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

The received wisdom is that summer skin is oily, humid, needs lighter products. The actual biology: UV radiation accelerates ceramide degradation in the stratum corneum. The barrier is not stronger in summer — it is more compromised. This post challenges the "oily summer skin" narrative with the mechanism it ignores.

**What the reader must feel:** The specific discomfort of realising that a piece of received skincare wisdom they have been acting on is wrong. Not self-critical — just reoriented.
