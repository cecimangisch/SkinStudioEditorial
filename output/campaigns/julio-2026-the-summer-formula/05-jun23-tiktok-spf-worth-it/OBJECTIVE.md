## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

The premium SPF market operates on an implicit promise: better formula, better protection. This post tests that promise with a specific comparison — an €8 EU-available mineral SPF (Altruist) versus a €45 chemical filter SPF (La Roche-Posay Anthelios, or equivalent premium). The finding: the SPF number is equally achievable at both price points. What varies is texture, cosmetic elegance, and compliance probability. The expensive SPF is not more protective — it is more wearable.

The editorial conclusion: if compliance is the variable that determines real-world protection, and an expensive SPF improves compliance, the price may be justified — but not for the reason the marketing implies.

**What the reader must feel:** The specific relief of understanding a confusing category. The desire to share because this resolves an argument they have had.
