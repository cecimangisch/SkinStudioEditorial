## EDITORIAL LOGIC

Summer is not a mood board. It is a set of conditions: increased UV radiation, heat, humidity, sweat, altered barrier function, photooxidation of active ingredients. This campaign treats those conditions with the same editorial rigour we apply to any formulation story.

Instagram and TikTok run parallel editorial tracks — not the same content adapted. Instagram is the authority record: deep carousels, INCI audits, saves. TikTok is the cold-audience hook: provocations, math, mechanism in 3–5 slides for a viewer who has never heard of us.
