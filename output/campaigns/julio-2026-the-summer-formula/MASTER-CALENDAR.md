## Editorial Logic

Summer is not a mood board. It is a set of conditions: increased UV radiation, heat, humidity, sweat, altered barrier function, photooxidation of active ingredients. This campaign treats those conditions with the same editorial rigour we apply to any formulation story. No seasonal "switches." No "summer routine." Just the mechanism — explained.

The column mix is weighted toward authority content (Ingredient School x3, The Label x2, The Verdict x1) with provocation anchors to drive reach. One brand study (Medik8) timed to mid-campaign to attract PR interest. Two Worth The Price? posts for virality and saves.

## Posting Cadence Notes

- Mon/Wed/Fri rhythm in weeks 3–4 for consistent engagement build.
- Week 1: Open with provocation (SPF math), follow immediately with depth (Zinc Oxide Exactly). This is the pattern: challenge the assumption, then provide the mechanism.
- Week 2: Label analysis (Altruist) anchors the SPF thread. Barrier provocation pivots from protection to function.
- Week 3: Niacinamide and Anthelios carry the authority load. Ceramides closes the barrier trilogy.
- Week 4: Myth provocation, CeraVe label, Vitamin C price comparison. Medik8 brand study for PR pull. Campaign closes with the anti-aging provocation — which is, in fact, about SPF.
