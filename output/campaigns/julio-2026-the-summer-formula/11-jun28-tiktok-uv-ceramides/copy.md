## TIKTOK CAPTION

Most people think of UV damage as a surface event — burning, tanning, redness. The structural damage is less visible and more significant.

UV radiation (specifically UVB) reduces ceramide synthesis in the stratum corneum and degrades existing lipids in the lipid matrix. Ceramides make up approximately 50% of the stratum corneum. When that fraction drops, barrier integrity drops with it. TEWL increases. The skin becomes more reactive to temperature, product ingredients, and environmental change.

SPF is essential — but it prevents further degradation. It does not replenish what UV has already removed. That requires a different intervention.
