## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

Most people understand UV as a tanning or burning mechanism. Fewer understand that UV directly degrades the lipid matrix of the stratum corneum — specifically the ceramide component that holds the skin barrier together. This post delivers that mechanism in four slides. It is preparation content for the ceramide series: the reader who understands why UV damages ceramides will understand why replenishing them matters.

**What the reader must feel:** That UV does something they hadn't considered — structural, not cosmetic. Saves because this is reference-level information presented concisely.
