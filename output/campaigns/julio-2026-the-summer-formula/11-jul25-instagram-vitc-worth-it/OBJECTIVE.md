# OBJECTIVE — 11 · Worth The Price?: €12 Vitamin C vs €85 Vitamin C
## Worth The Price? · July 25, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
Vitamin C is one of the most price-stratified product categories in skincare — and also one of the most misunderstood. The €12 option (The Ordinary Ascorbic Acid 8% + Alpha Arbutin 2%) and the €85 option (SkinCeuticals CE Ferulic) are genuinely different formulations targeting different use cases. The comparison centers on three variables that actually determine vitamin C product efficacy: form of vitamin C used, concentration, and stability. The campaign's summer angle: vitamin C degrades faster in heat and light — which changes the value calculation for expensive, unstable formulas.

**Editorial Note:**
SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic is genuinely differentiated. The 15% L-ascorbic acid at pH 2.5–3.5 with ferulic acid and vitamin E is the most clinically studied vitamin C formulation commercially available. The price reflects real formulation R&D and clinical trial investment. The honest comparison is: The Ordinary is effective at its concentration and far more affordable; SkinCeuticals performs at a higher level but at a price that requires considered use. Neither is the wrong answer — the answer depends on budget and intent.
