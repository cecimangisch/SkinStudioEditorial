Vitamin C is the most purchased skincare active after SPF. Most buyers do not know that L-ascorbic acid (the active form) oxidises on exposure to air, heat, and light — and that a serum that has turned yellow-orange is already degraded. The provocation: the product the reader is using may not be working. This is preparation content for the Worth It? comparison (posts 19/20): if the reader doesn't know Vitamin C oxidises, the price comparison is missing its context.

## EDITOR IN CHIEF AUDIT NOTES

The claim must be precise: L-ascorbic acid oxidises. Not all Vitamin C derivatives do so at the same rate (ascorbyl glucoside, sodium ascorbyl phosphate are more stable). Do not generalise to all Vitamin C forms. The post is about L-ascorbic acid specifically — the form in most widely marketed serums.
