## TIKTOK CAPTION

L-ascorbic acid is the most biologically active form of Vitamin C in skincare. It is also highly unstable. Exposure to air, heat, and UV light triggers oxidation — turning the serum yellow, then orange, then brown. At that point, the active has largely degraded.

Most Vitamin C serums are sold in clear bottles, stored in bathrooms (warm, humid, light-exposed), and used over two or three months after opening. The product works as advertised in controlled conditions. Real-world storage conditions are often not that.

What stability actually requires: pH around 3.5, opaque or airless packaging, cool and dark storage. These are formulation and packaging decisions — not marketing ones.
