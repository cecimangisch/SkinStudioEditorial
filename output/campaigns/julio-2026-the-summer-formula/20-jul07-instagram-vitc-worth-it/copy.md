## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

The SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic formula was built on a specific finding: 15% L-ascorbic acid, alpha-tocopherol, and ferulic acid at pH 3.5. Ferulic acid was key — it doubles the photoprotective efficacy of the Vitamin C and significantly improves stability against oxidation. That combination was patented. The patent expired in 2012.

The Ordinary's formulation achieves the same mechanism at 8% L-ascorbic acid and ferulic acid, for €15. The pH is controlled. The active works. The price difference is real.

What the €50 premium buys: higher concentration (15% has more clinical documentation than 8%), packaging engineered for stability at higher L-ascorbic acid concentrations, and the research legacy of the original formulation.

Both are defensible choices. The variable is what matters to you — mechanism at accessible price, or clinical-dose with full research backing.

Read the label. Choose accordingly.
