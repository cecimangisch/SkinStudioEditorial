## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

La Roche-Posay Anthelios gets recommended constantly. Its formula gets explained almost never. This is the INCI audit.

The key differentiator is Mexoryl XL — Drometrizole Trisiloxane — which provides UVA1 protection. UVA1 is the deepest UV wavelength range. It contributes to both tanning and photoaging. Most broad-spectrum SPFs, including many marketed as high-protection, do not cover it. This one does.

Paired with Mexoryl SX for the UVA2-UVB bridge, stabilised by Octocrylene, supported by silica for sebum control and a film-forming system that makes reapplication realistic.

Lab Score: INVEST. Not because of the brand. Because of what's in the formula that others typically omit.
