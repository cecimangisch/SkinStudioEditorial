# OBJECTIVE — 10 · The Label: CeraVe AM Facial Moisturising Lotion SPF 25
## The Label · July 23, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
CeraVe AM is one of the most widely recommended combination moisturiser-SPF products globally. This post performs a complete INCI analysis and evaluates whether the product delivers on both its moisturising (ceramide) and SPF claims — and whether the SPF 25 level is adequate for summer use. The verdict is nuanced: excellent moisturising base, credible ceramide formulation, but SPF 25 is below the minimum recommended for face (SPF 30–50) by most dermatological bodies in summer conditions. This distinction is worth stating clearly.

**Editorial Note:**
SPF 25 is not "bad." It blocks approximately 96% of UVB. But in summer, when UV index regularly exceeds 6, most dermatological guidance recommends SPF 30 minimum — and SPF 50 for high exposure. This product is best positioned as a low-UV-index daily moisturiser, not a summer beach-day formula. That is a legitimate editorial distinction.
