## SLIDE 3 — INCI: UV Filters

**Background:** Ink #1A1A18
**Text:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** The UV filters.

**Body:**
**Zinc Oxide (9.4%):** mineral UV filter. Broad-spectrum: UVB, UVA2, UVA1. Photostable. Anti-inflammatory.

**Octinoxate (Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate, 6%):** chemical UVB filter. Effective UVB coverage but limited UVA. Photounstable — but UVB is the primary target here.

This is a hybrid mineral-chemical filter system. The zinc oxide provides UVA coverage; octinoxate enhances UVB protection.

SPF 25 assessment: at 9.4% zinc oxide + 6% octinoxate, SPF 25 is the achieved result. To reach SPF 50, the formulation would require higher filter concentrations — which would change the texture.

**Note:** octinoxate is one of the filters flagged in some studies for potential hormonal disruption. EU regulations permit it; some users prefer zinc-only formulas.

## SLIDE 4 — INCI: Ceramide System

**Background:** Lab Mist #DAE9DF

**Headline:** The ceramide system.

**Body:**
**Ceramide NP, Ceramide AP, Ceramide EOP:** three ceramide types, addressing different aspects of the lipid bilayer structure. The combination reflects the 'restore the full lipid matrix' approach, not a single-ceramide shortcut.

**Cholesterol:** lipid bilayer co-component. Correctly paired with ceramides.

**Phytosphingosine:** precursor to ceramide synthesis. Supports endogenous ceramide production.

The MVE technology (MultiVesicular Emulsion) encapsulates ceramides in a multilayer sphere for gradual release over time — the mechanism behind the "24-hour hydration" claim.

Ceramide system assessment: credible. Multi-ceramide with cholesterol and ceramide precursor. MVE technology has clinical support.

## SLIDE 5 — INCI: Hydrating and Skin-Conditioning Agents

**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** The hydrating agents.

**Body:**
**Hyaluronic Acid (Sodium Hyaluronate):** humectant. Attracts water to the stratum corneum. Molecular weight determines penetration depth — product does not specify, which is standard practice.

**Niacinamide:** vitamin B3. Sebum regulation, barrier support, anti-inflammatory. At the concentration in this formula (~2%), the sebum-regulating and barrier-supporting effects are the primary benefit.

**Glycerin:** humectant. Draws water to skin surface. Works synergistically with hyaluronic acid.

Assessment: standard but functional humectant system. Well-tolerated across skin types.

## SLIDE 7 — Verdict

**Background:** Lab Sage #BBD1C6

**Verdict:** WORTH IT — WITH ONE CAVEAT

**Body:**
The ceramide formulation is credible. The multi-ceramide + cholesterol system with MVE technology is the real product achievement. The humectant stack (hyaluronic acid, glycerin, niacinamide) is effective and well-tolerated. No fragrance. Non-comedogenic.

The caveat: SPF 25 is below the SPF 30–50 range recommended for summer facial use by most dermatological bodies when UV index is above 3 (which is most summer days).

Use case: excellent as a daily moisturiser with incidental sun exposure (indoor days, winter, low-UV months). For summer outdoor use, layer with SPF 50 over the top.

## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

CeraVe AM has a credible ceramide system. The SPF 25 is the one honest limitation worth discussing.


The ceramide formulation in CeraVe AM is legitimate: Ceramide NP, AP, and EOP — three types addressing different aspects of the lipid bilayer — paired with cholesterol and phytosphingosine (a ceramide precursor). The MVE technology has clinical support for sustained release. The humectant stack (sodium hyaluronate, glycerin, niacinamide) is standard and effective. No fragrance, non-comedogenic.

The UV filter system: zinc oxide (9.4%) + octinoxate (6%). Hybrid mineral-chemical. Achieves SPF 25. Most dermatological guidance recommends SPF 30 minimum for daily facial use in summer when UV index is above 3.

SPF 25 blocks 96% of UVB. SPF 50 blocks 98%. The gap is smaller than it sounds — but the recommendation gap is real.

Best use case: year-round daily moisturiser with incidental exposure. For active summer days, apply SPF 50 over the top.
