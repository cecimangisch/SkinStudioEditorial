## SLIDE 2

**Background:** Lab Frost #ECF0EE

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):**
What heat actually does to your skin.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
Heat increases transepidermal water loss (TEWL). Your barrier is losing water faster than usual — even if you can't feel it.

Simultaneously: sebum production increases under heat. Eccrine sweat (the kind that regulates temperature) disrupts the skin's acid mantle.

The result is a compromised barrier — more reactive, more permeable, more prone to congestion.

Your moisturiser did not cause this. Stopping it will not fix it. A compromised barrier with no moisturiser is simply a compromised barrier with less protection.

The problem is not your product. The problem is the season.

## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Heat increases transepidermal water loss. Your barrier is compromised before you've put anything on your face.


In summer, two things happen simultaneously: heat increases TEWL (transepidermal water loss), and eccrine sweat disrupts the acid mantle — the skin's natural pH-regulating surface. Sebum production also rises under heat. The result is a barrier that is more permeable, more reactive, and more prone to congestion.

People conclude the moisturiser is the problem and stop using it. But a disrupted barrier with no moisturiser is the same disrupted barrier with less protection. The congestion continues.

What actually helps: a lighter-textured moisturiser with barrier-supporting ingredients (ceramides, niacinamide, fatty acids), applied to damp skin, with SPF over the top.

The formulation matters. The category does not.
