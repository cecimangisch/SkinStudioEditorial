# OBJECTIVE — 04 · Barrier Summer
## Open/Provocation · July 9, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
Challenge the common belief that moisturiser is causing summer breakouts. The actual mechanism is sweat-induced barrier disruption combined with increased sebum production under heat — not the moisturiser formulation. This post pivots the campaign from UV protection to barrier function, setting up the Niacinamide and Ceramides posts that follow.

**Column:** Open/Provocation
**Slides:** 2
**Hook:** "Your moisturiser isn't breaking you out. The summer is."

**Target Audience:**

Secondary: Skincare educators, dermatology content accounts.

**Success Metrics:**
- Comments: personal responses ("this is literally me"), saves, shares
- Target shares: >2% of reach (this is the kind of post people send to friends)

**Editorial Note:**
The mechanism is: heat increases transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and alters the lipid matrix of the stratum corneum. Simultaneously, eccrine sweat disrupts the acid mantle. These two factors, not the moisturiser, drive the "summer breakout" cycle. The moisturiser is often the only thing preventing it from being worse.
