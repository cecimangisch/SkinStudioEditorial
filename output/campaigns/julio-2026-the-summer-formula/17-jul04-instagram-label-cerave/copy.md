## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

CeraVe AM is positioned as a moisturiser with SPF and ceramides. The INCI list tells a more specific story.

The UV filter system — Homosalate, Octisalate, Octocrylene — provides strong UVB coverage and functional UVA protection. It does not include Mexoryl, Tinosorb, or Avobenzone. For everyday urban use, the coverage is adequate. For beach days and extended sun exposure, it is not sufficient.

The three ceramides (NP, AP, EOP) are present and real. They sit mid-list, which means this is primarily formulated as an SPF product. The ceramide fraction supports the barrier without defining the formula.

Niacinamide and Sodium Hyaluronate appear toward the end of the list — supporting actives at lower concentrations.

Lab Score: SAVE. The best-value ceramide-plus-SPF combination on the market. Know what it is, and what it isn't.
