## EDITOR IN CHIEF AUDIT NOTES

The UVA note requires precision: chemical filters provide UVA-A coverage via Avobenzone-adjacent mechanism. CeraVe AM does not contain Butyl Methoxydibenzoylmethane (Avobenzone) or Mexoryl — the UVA coverage is principally from Octocrylene (partial) and Octisalate (limited UVA). State this as a scope limitation, not a deficiency for its intended everyday use case.
