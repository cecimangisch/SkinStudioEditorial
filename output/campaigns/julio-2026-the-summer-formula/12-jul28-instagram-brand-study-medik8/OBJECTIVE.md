# OBJECTIVE — 12 · Brand Study: Understanding Medik8
## Brand Study · July 28, 2026

**Editorial Objective:**
Medik8 is a UK-founded professional skincare brand that has grown significantly through dermatology clinic distribution. Its positioning — "SEE, PROTECT, PREVENT" (vitamin C, SPF, retinol) — is one of the cleaner brand philosophies in the market. This post analyses the brand through an editorial lens: its formulation approach, hero ingredients, clinical positioning, and whether the brand's claims are supported by its product architecture. The summer angle: Medik8's vitamin C and SPF line are central to the summer formula brief.
