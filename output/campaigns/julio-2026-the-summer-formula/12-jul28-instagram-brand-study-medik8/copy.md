## SLIDE 2 — Origin and Philosophy

**Background:** Lab Frost #ECF0EE

**Headline:** Origin and philosophy.

**Body:**
Medik8 was founded in 2009 in the UK by pharmacist Elliot Isaacs. It was conceived as a professional-grade range — initially sold exclusively through dermatologists, cosmetic clinics, and aesthetic practitioners. Direct-to-consumer distribution came later.

The founding philosophy: "SEE, PROTECT, PREVENT" — three-pillar routine built around vitamin C (antioxidant morning), SPF (photoprotection), and retinol (cellular turnover evening). This maps directly to the routine with the strongest combined clinical evidence for long-term skin health.

The brand operates at the intersection of pharmaceutical rigour and cosmetics — which explains both its pricing and its clinical credibility.

## SLIDE 3 — Hero Ingredient Approach: Vitamin C

**Background:** Lab Mist #DAE9DF

**Headline:** Hero ingredients: vitamin C.

**Body:**
Medik8's vitamin C range uses a multi-form strategy. The C-Tetra range uses tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate (THD ascorbate) — a fat-soluble vitamin C ester that is more stable than L-ascorbic acid and penetrates the lipid-rich skin environment differently.

The Fresh C range and Vitamin C line use L-ascorbic acid — less stable but the most biologically studied form.

The strategic choice: Medik8 acknowledges that different vitamin C forms have different stability and delivery profiles, and offers both. This is evidence of genuine formulation philosophy rather than marketing simplification.

Key question for consumers: THD ascorbate is more stable but has less direct clinical evidence than LAA. For summer, stability may matter more than raw potency.

## SLIDE 4 — Hero Ingredient Approach: SPF

**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** Hero ingredients: SPF.

**Body:**
Medik8's Advanced Day Total Protect SPF 50 combines Tinosorb M and Tinosorb S (both EU-approved modern filters not yet FDA-approved) with zinc oxide and additional UV filters for full-spectrum coverage. Photostable. No whitening.

The Clean Screen range uses a zinc oxide-only mineral approach — for users who prefer physical-only formulas.

Both lines address the practical limitation of most SPF products: either performance compromised for texture (mineral), or texture-first but filter questions (older chemical systems). Medik8 offers a third option: modern photostable chemical filters with cosmetically elegant results.

## SLIDE 5 — Hero Ingredient Approach: Retinol

**Background:** Lab Frost #ECF0EE

**Headline:** Hero ingredients: retinol.

**Body:**
Medik8's Crystal Retinal range uses retinaldehyde (retinal) rather than retinol. Retinal is one step closer in the conversion pathway to retinoic acid (the active form) — meaning it requires one fewer enzymatic conversion step in skin than retinol.

Clinical data suggests retinal is approximately 11× more potent than retinol, with faster visible results. It is also more likely to cause irritation at equivalent concentrations.

The concentration range: Crystal Retinal 1 (0.01%), 3 (0.03%), 6 (0.06%), 10 (0.1%), 20 (0.2%). The step-up system addresses the irritation risk — a formulation design choice informed by the retinoic acid conversion model.

## SLIDE 6 — Formulation Architecture

**Background:** Ink #1A1A18
**Text:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline:** Formulation architecture.

**Body:**
What distinguishes Medik8 from most skincare brands is the internal formulation coherence. The "SEE, PROTECT, PREVENT" framework is not just marketing — it is the clinical routine with the best evidence, built into the brand's product architecture.

Vitamin C in the morning: antioxidant protection against UV-induced free radical damage before photoprotection.
SPF over the top: primary photoprotection.
Retinol in the evening: cellular turnover, collagen stimulation, long-term structural intervention.

Each step addresses a different mechanism. The three mechanisms together cover the complete evidence-based approach to long-term skin health. Very few brands organise around this logic with the formula quality to support it.
