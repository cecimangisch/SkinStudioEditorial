## EDITOR IN CHIEF AUDIT NOTES

The "40 years of data" claim refers to longitudinal cohort studies on photoaging prevention with UV filters — including the Nambour study and subsequent replication. State it as "40 years of data," not "proven to reverse aging." SPF prevents UV-induced photoaging — it does not reverse existing damage. Accurate claim, accurate framing.
