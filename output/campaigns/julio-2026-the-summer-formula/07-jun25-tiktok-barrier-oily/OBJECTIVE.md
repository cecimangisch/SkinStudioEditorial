## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

The conflation of oiliness and overactive sebum production is one of skincare's most persistent misunderstandings. In summer, what reads as "oilier skin" is frequently a compromised barrier — lipid depletion from UV, heat, and increased sweating — triggering a compensatory sebum response. The reader who identifies as "oily-skinned in summer" is the target. The reframe is mechanical: the problem is the barrier, not the sebaceous gland.

This is a three-slide provocation. S1 disrupts a held belief. S2 provides the mechanism. S3 provides the redirect — toward repair, not control.

**What the reader must feel:** The discomfort of a belief they held being made more precise. The motivation to follow because this account explains things others don't.
