## TIKTOK CAPTION

Your skin isn't producing more oil in summer. It is responding to barrier damage.

UV radiation and heat deplete the lipid matrix of the stratum corneum. When barrier integrity drops, transepidermal water loss increases. The skin's response to that: compensatory sebum production. It is a defense mechanism, not a skin type.

Treating summer oiliness as an excess production problem — with mattifying products, alcohol toners, aggressive acids — addresses the response while leaving the cause intact. The result is a cycle: strip, compensate, strip again.

The mechanism requires repair. Ceramide replenishment. Reduced friction. SPF that doesn't require heavy removal.
