## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

Summer skincare guides tell you to swap your routine. Your barrier has different advice.


In summer, three things happen simultaneously: UV exposure degrades ceramides. Heat increases transepidermal water loss. Sweat and increased cleansing disrupt the acid mantle. These are barrier events, not routine events.

What your skin needs: ceramides and fatty acids to replace what UV and cleansing remove. Niacinamide to regulate sebum and support ceramide synthesis. SPF applied at the correct quantity and reapplied. A cleanser that doesn't extract more lipids than it needs to.

A lighter texture doesn't mean a lighter barrier. An SPF you actually use outperforms a higher-factor one you avoid because of the finish. An understood routine beats a seasonal one.

The variable that changes is the stressor. The principle doesn't.
