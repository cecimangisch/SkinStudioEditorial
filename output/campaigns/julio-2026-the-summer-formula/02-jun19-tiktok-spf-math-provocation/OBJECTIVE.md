## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

The SPF 30 vs SPF 50 number gap is the most misunderstood fact in consumer sunscreen. Most people believe SPF 50 provides roughly double the protection of SPF 30. The actual difference: 97% vs 98% of UVB filtered. One percentage point.

This post does one thing: states that fact, shows the math, and lets the implication land without comment. The provocation is in the number. The post does not tell the reader what to think — it shows them the data they have not been shown.

**What the reader must feel:** Mild cognitive dissonance. The number on the bottle has meant something different to them than it should. This feeling is what drives the share.
