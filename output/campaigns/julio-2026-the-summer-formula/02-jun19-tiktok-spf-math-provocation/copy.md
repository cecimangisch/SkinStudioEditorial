## TIKTOK CAPTION

SPF 30 blocks 97% of UVB. SPF 50 blocks 98%. The difference is 1%.

Most people assume SPF 50 provides roughly twice the protection of SPF 30. That is not how the scale works. SPF measures the ratio of UV dose needed to cause sunburn — not a linear percentage improvement.

What actually determines protection: how much product you apply, how often you reapply, and whether you cover all exposed skin.

The number matters less than the habit.
