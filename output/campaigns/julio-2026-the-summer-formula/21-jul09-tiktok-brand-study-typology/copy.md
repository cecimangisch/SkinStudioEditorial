## TIKTOK CAPTION

Most skincare brands list active ingredients without stating their concentration. You see "niacinamide" on the label. You don't know if it's 2% or 10%. You're buying a marketing claim.

Typology, founded in France in 2019, publishes the concentration of every active on the label and their website. Niacinamide 10%. Retinol 0.5%. Hyaluronic Acid serum. Each percentage is stated. No fragrance as default. Clinical testing across the range. Price point €10–25.

The limitation: some actives sit at the lower end of the clinically studied range. But the transparency means you can evaluate that yourself.
