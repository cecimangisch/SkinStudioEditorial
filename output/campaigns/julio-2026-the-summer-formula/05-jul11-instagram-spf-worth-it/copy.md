**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** €8 · Altruist Mineral SPF 50

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
**UV filters:** Zinc oxide + titanium dioxide
**Filter type:** Mineral (physical)
**Spectrum:** Broad-spectrum. Full UVA1 coverage via zinc oxide.

**Texture:** Thicker. White cast visible on medium-dark skin tones.

## SLIDE 5 — Product B continued

**Background:** Bone #F5F0E8

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** €45 · What the price buys.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
The Mexoryl filter system (proprietary to L'Oréal) represents genuine formulation R&D. Mexoryl SX is one of the best UVA1 filters available and is not replicable without L'Oréal's patents.

**What you are paying for:**
Advanced filter technology with superior UVA1 performance. Fluid texture that disappears on all skin tones. A formula designed to be worn under makeup without pilling. No white cast. Formulation aesthetics that drive consistent daily use.

**What you are not paying for:**
Better UVB protection. The SPF 50+ number provides marginally more UVB blocking than SPF 50 — the difference is less than 1%.

## SLIDE 6 — Verdict

**Background:** Lab Sage #BBD1C6

**Headline (DM Sans Bold):** The verdict.

**Body (DM Sans Regular):**
**For daily SPF use on fair-to-medium skin tones:** The €8 option provides equivalent UVB protection and adequate UVA coverage. Worth It.

**For daily SPF use on medium-dark to dark skin tones:** The mineral formula's white cast is a real barrier to consistent use. The €45 formula's aesthetics are functionally meaningful here.

**For extended outdoor exposure:** The Mexoryl system's UVA1 depth is the difference. The €45 price gap is justified for beach, sport, or high-altitude use.

The price difference does not buy better numbers. It buys better compliance — and compliance is the variable that actually determines how protected you are.
