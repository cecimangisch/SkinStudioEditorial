## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

We audited 13 verified May-June 2026 skincare launches. Here's what the INCI lists reveal — and whether the marketing is telling the truth.

**The Pattern:** Big brands don't always win on formula. Obagi's vitamin C lacks photostable antioxidant backup. Lancôme splits actives across three products (less concentration per step). INKEY's oat balm prioritizes texture over barrier occlusion. Jurlique stayed botanical, skipped clinical actives. These aren't failures — they're formula gaps that precision reveals.

**The Winners:** The Ordinary (transparency + actives at entry price). Cetaphil (day/night protocols, no active conflict). Botnia + EADEM (sunscreen innovation — no white cast, reapplication over makeup). PCA SKIN (triple exfoliation with gentler mechanics). ILIA (tinted mineral serum SPF).

**What this means for summer 2026:** Formula-first skincare wins. Photostability is non-negotiable. Sunscreen is the first active. Exfoliation works better when it's clinically precise, not aggressive.

Before you buy the next "hero product," audit the INCI. Ask: Concentration? Photostable? Does it work with your other actives?

That's how you build a summer routine that actually works — because the formula proves it, not because a brand told you to.
