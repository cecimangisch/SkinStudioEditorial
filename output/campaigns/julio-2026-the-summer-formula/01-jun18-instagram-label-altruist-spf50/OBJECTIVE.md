## EDITORIAL ANGLE (CEO)

Altruist Mineral SPF 50 is the most-discussed accessible mineral sunscreen in the EU market. At approximately €3–5 for 200ml, it is either a genuine formulation story or a cheap product that gets charity clicks. This post answers that question with the INCI list, not with brand sentiment. The Label audits — it does not review.

The audience must feel that they are reading a formulation report, not a product recommendation. The verdict emerges from the data. The data is in the INCI list.

**What the reader must leave with:** An accurate understanding of what zinc oxide and titanium dioxide do at a formulation level, whether the claims on the packaging are supported, and a specific, measurable limitation (whitening on deeper skin tones — a particle size issue, not a safety concern).
