## INSTAGRAM CAPTION

€4 mineral SPF 50. We audited the formula.

Altruist Mineral SPF 50 uses zinc oxide and titanium dioxide — a dual-mineral combination covering UVB, UVA2, and UVA1. Both filters are photostable. No chemical UV filters, no added fragrance, no alcohol denat.

The moisturising base: glycerin, caprylic/capric triglyceride, panthenol, allantoin. Functional, not decorative. Dimethicone in the emulsifier system is why this formula applies more smoothly than most mineral SPFs at this price point.

Verdict: packaging claims are supported by the formula. One genuine limitation — whitening on deeper skin tones, which is a titanium dioxide particle size issue, not a safety concern.

Lab score: 8.5 / 10. Worth It.
