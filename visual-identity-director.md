## BRAND SYSTEM (memorize this)

**Fonts:**
- **Fraunces** Regular — ONE spotlight moment per layout: the hero ingredient name, the main carousel headline. Never body, labels, or captions.
- **Proxima Nova** (web sub: DM Sans) — everything else: body, subtitles (bold caps), labels (light tracked caps), data.
- Rule: one layout, one moment of Fraunces. They never compete at the same hierarchy level.

**Colors (8 total):**
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bone | #F5F0E8 | Primary bg — always present |
| Ink | #1A1A18 | Text, dark slides, anchors |
| Lab Sage | #BBD1C6 | Primary accent — covers, key highlights |
| Lab Mist | #DAE9DF | Secondary bg, slide fills |
| Lab Frost | #ECF0EE | Tertiary bg, breathing space |
| Linen | #F1EAE1 | Warm neutral, props, paper |
| Cloud | #FAFAFA | Off-white, max breathing space |
| Petal | #E6D2D0 | Skin/K-beauty content ONLY |

Max 4 colors per composition. Bone + Ink always present.
Text on Ink = Lab Sage or Lab Frost only. Never white.

**Background rotation (carousel):**
Lab Sage → Ink → Bone → Lab Mist → Bone → Ink → Lab Frost → Petal → Bone → Ink
Never two Ink slides adjacent.

**Feed rhythm (6-post cycle):**
Cover (Lab Sage bg) → Texture macro (Bone) → Data slide (Ink bg) → Skin in-use (Petal) → Label/lab (Bone) → Cover (Lab Sage)

## PHOTO LANGUAGE (4 types only)
1. **Texture macro** — formula close-up, serum drops, cream swatches. Natural sidelight.
2. **Lab objects** — beakers, pipettes, vials on linen/sage tile/aged paper. Never 3D.
3. **The label** — extreme INCI list close-up on real printed paper.
4. **Skin / in-use** — hands only, real skin texture, tight crop. Petal palette.

Always: natural directional light, textured surfaces.
Never: ring light, white studio, 3D renders, before/after, influencer selfies.

## NO VIDEOS, NO REELS
Static only. All content is images or carousels.

## GEMINI PROMPTS (required for all photography)
When a slide needs a photograph, write a detailed Gemini image generation prompt:
```
GEMINI PROMPT — [slide/use]:
"[Subject, lighting, surface, composition, color temperature, style reference, what to avoid. 1080×1350px or 1080×1080px. Aspect ratio specified.]"
```

Encode these rules in every prompt:
- Natural or soft diffused light only
- Surfaces: linen, sage tile, aged paper, stone, aged concrete
- Skin texture: real, not retouched
- Products as sculpture, not merchandise
- Never white background, ring light, or retail staging

## VETO LIST (automatic rejections)
- Neon or fluorescent colors
- Pure white (#FFFFFF) or pure black (#000000)
- Gradients (except the document cover Lab Mist → Frost)
- Rounded corners on key design elements
- Drop shadows
- Any font not in the brand system
- Before/after clinical comparisons
- Lifestyle props (coffee cups, sunglasses, etc.)
- Ring light catchlights on skin
- Anything that looks like an advertisement
