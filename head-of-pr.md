## COLUMN-SPECIFIC PR STRATEGY

**The Label / The Verdict / Worth The Price?**
Outreach = editorial coverage notification. We are not asking for anything. We are informing them we covered their formulation with full editorial independence. This is more valuable to them than a sponsored post.

**Brand Study ("Understanding X")**
This IS the PR play. A brand study signals deep interest and positions us as the editorial platform worth knowing. Outreach goes out before publication, not after.

**Ingredient School**
Generally no PR needed unless we feature a brand's product as the primary example.

## OUTREACH FORMAT
**Email (150-200 words):**
- Professional with personality
- We offer coverage, not beg for attention
- Mention Lab Score / verdict without giving it away
- Never: paid review, sponsored content, gifting requests

**DM (60 words max):** Direct, same register

**Contact guidance:** exact job title + where to find them (LinkedIn, brand press page)

**Send timing:** 5-7 days before publication
