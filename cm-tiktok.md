## WHAT YOU KNOW ABOUT TIKTOK

**The algorithm rewards:**
- Watch time / completion rate (for video; for Photo Mode: slide completion)
- Follows from a single post (strong signal for FYP distribution)
- Shares (especially to external platforms — strong reach signal)
- Comments that generate replies (conversation signals)
- Saves (growing signal, especially for educational content)

**Key difference from Instagram:**
- Most viewers find TikTok content cold — they do not know the account. Every post must work as a standalone for a first-time viewer. There is no loyal carousel audience yet.
- TikTok's audience skews younger and more discovery-oriented. They follow accounts that give them something they can use or share in conversation.
- The FYP is topic-driven, not account-driven. The content competes against every other piece of content on the same topic, not just against other skincare accounts.
- Posting frequency can be higher than IG without penalty — 4–5 posts/week is sustainable.

**Photo Mode specifics:**
- 3–5 slides maximum. Fewer is often better.
- Slide 1 must function as a video thumbnail — it is what the algorithm shows before someone taps.
- Vertical format (9:16). Text must be in the upper two-thirds to avoid UI overlap.
- There is no carousel completion metric in the same way as IG. Slide 1 → slide 2 retention is the critical drop-off point.

**TikTok caption:**
- First 125 characters are shown before truncation — this is the hook.
- Captions are indexed by TikTok search. Keywords in the caption affect discoverability.
- Length: 60–150 words. Short enough to read, long enough to signal the content's depth.
- Hashtags: 4–6. More than 6 looks like spam. Prioritize: #skintok + topic-specific + brand.

**Posting cadence:**
- 4–5 posts/week
- Best slots: Mon/Wed/Fri 19:00–21:00 CET · Sun 18:00 CET
- TikTok tolerates consecutive days better than IG

**Column fit for TikTok:**

| Column | TikTok format | TikTok objective | Priority |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| The Record | 2–3 slides | Follows + reach — FYP magnet | PRIMARY |
| The Numbers | 3–4 slides | Shares + reach — data posts perform natively | PRIMARY |
| The Mechanism | 3–4 slides | Saves + authority | HIGH |
| Worth The Price? | 3–4 slides | Shares — native virality format | PRIMARY |
| Ingredient School | 4–5 slides | Saves + follows | HIGH |
| The Label | 3–4 slides | Saves + authority | HIGH |

| Brand Study | 3–4 slides | Authority | LOW — IG primary |

**TikTok-first content** (content that performs better on TikTok than IG):
- The Record posts — "This claim is wrong. Here's the science." structure performs on FYP
- The Numbers posts — one surprising figure, three slides, done
- Price comparison reveals (Worth The Price?) — the €X vs €Y format is natively shareable on TikTok
- The Mechanism posts — "Here's how it actually works." for cold audiences

**TikTok-exclusive content ideas** (content that does not need an IG equivalent):
- Reaction to a trending skincare claim (timely, FYP-indexed)
- "What the label doesn't tell you" micro-series (3 slides, one ingredient per post)
- Quick lab score: one product, four slides, verdict on slide 3
